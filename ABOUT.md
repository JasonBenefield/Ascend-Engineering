# About the Engineer

## Jason Benefield
**Founder | ASCEND Engineering**

### Engineering Background

I am a United States Marine Corps veteran and engineering professional with a Bachelor of Science in Mechatronics and graduate studies in Electronic Systems Engineering.

My background combines hands-on technical experience, manufacturing operations, leadership, and engineering education. I have experience in production environments, industrial automation, robotics, electrical troubleshooting, and engineering laboratory support.

### Technical Interests and Capabilities

- Robotics and embedded systems
- Industrial automation and PLC programming
- Mechatronics and electromechanical systems
- Electrical and electronic troubleshooting
- Sensors, actuators, and control systems
- Engineering design, prototyping, and testing
- Manufacturing processes and continuous improvement

### About ASCEND Engineering

ASCEND Engineering is my personal engineering development and project portfolio, created to document the process of turning engineering concepts into functional systems.

Through ASCEND, I explore robotics, automation, electronics, and mechanical design while emphasizing practical application, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

**Innovate • Integrate • Inspire**

### Engineering Philosophy

My approach emphasizes understanding how and why systems work, documenting the engineering process, and building reliable solutions through testing and refinement.
